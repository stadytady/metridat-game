THE HILL ABOVE THE STRAIT

Panticapaeum, capital of the Bosporan Kingdom, in the 4th century BCE. Walk down from the summit along a stepped street, past a potter at his wheel and hens in the yards, cross the agora, go down to a fisherman mending his net on the beach, and climb to the sanctuary of Hecate. Eight finds, each turnable in close-up, tell what archaeology knows: sourced history is kept apart from the game's own fiction.

Every find brings the evening on; the ships come in to anchor. At night, light the lamps along the way, lie down in Hecate's abaton, and follow the dream into the fog.

Low-poly, 640×360, wobbling vertices, affine textures, 15-bit dither: built to look like a lost archaeology game from 1999. All assets are original and generated in code.
